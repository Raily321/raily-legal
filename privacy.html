This Privacy Policy explains how Raily ("Raily," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Raily iOS application (the "App"). Raily is a family drying rail slot tracker and laundry pickup inventory tool designed to work primarily on your device.

Short summary. Raily is local-first and offline-capable. Your rail, batch, move, and pickup records stay on your device. We do not require an account. Optional camera, microphone, photo library, and speech features run only when you choose them. App Tracking Transparency and notification prompts may appear for system compliance, but Raily does not use them for advertising or marketing push campaigns. In-app coins are StoreKit consumable purchases processed by Apple.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Raily iOS App and related support contact at support@raily.app. It does not apply to third-party websites, services, or Apple platforms that you may reach through system share sheets or App Store purchases.

2. No account; local-first storage

  • Raily does not create user accounts, profiles, or cloud logins.
  • Rail sections, laundry batches, move events, pickup inventory, notes, and optional media attachments are stored locally on your device.
  • The App is designed to work offline for core recording and review features.
  • If you delete the App, or erase device storage without a backup you control, local data may be permanently lost. Device backups follow Apple's own backup settings and are outside our control.

3. Information the App may process on your device

Depending on how you use Raily, the App may process:

  • Content you enter: labels, counts, timestamps, zones, notes, and similar inventory fields.
  • Optional media: photos you take or select to attach as references.
  • Optional speech input: audio processed on demand only when you use speech features to dictate text; we do not continuously record audio.
  • Purchase receipts for coins: handled through Apple StoreKit on your Apple ID; see Section 6.
  • Diagnostic signals from Apple: if you opt in to share App analytics with developers through iOS settings, Apple may provide aggregated crash or usage metrics. Raily does not operate its own advertising analytics stack.

4. Permissions (on-demand only)

Raily requests sensitive permissions only when you start a related action, not as a condition of opening the App for basic text-based recording.

  • Camera: used only when you choose to take an optional reference photo for a record on this device.
  • Photo library / system photo picker: used only when you choose existing images to attach. The App receives the items you select; it does not scan your entire library in the background.
  • Microphone: used only when you enable an on-demand speech or audio capture feature that requires it.
  • Speech recognition: used only when you explicitly start speech-to-text or similar dictation for a field you are editing.

If you deny a permission, you can continue using other App features that do not need that permission (for example, text-only records). You can change permissions later in iOS Settings.

5. App Tracking Transparency and notifications

  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT): iOS may present a tracking authorization prompt. Raily does not use ATT authorization for advertising, cross-app tracking, or selling personal data. Any prompt is for system compliance and transparency, not to power ads.
  • Notifications: iOS may ask for notification permission. Raily does not use notification permission for advertising push, marketing campaigns, or promotional blasts. If notifications are enabled in a future optional reminder workflow that you control, they would be for your own inventory reminders—not ads.

6. StoreKit consumable coin purchases

  • Optional in-app coins are sold as Apple StoreKit consumable products.
  • Payment processing, billing, refunds, and Apple ID payment data are handled by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy practices.
  • We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Coin balances and related entitlements needed to unlock optional features are managed in connection with the App and Apple's purchase infrastructure on your device / Apple account context.
  • Consumable coins are generally not restorable like non-consumable unlocks; check the product description in the App and Apple's purchase rules before buying.

7. Export and sharing

  • Export is initiated only by you.
  • When you export, Raily can prepare a local file package (for example structured data and any media you include) and present the iOS system share sheet.
  • You choose the destination (Files, AirDrop, another app, etc.). Closing the share sheet does not mean a recipient received the file.
  • We do not upload your export automatically to our servers when you open a detail screen or statistics view.

8. What we do not do

  • No required account or social login.
  • No advertising network integration for serving ads based on your laundry records.
  • No sale of your personal inventory content.
  • No silent cloud sync of your rail history to a Raily-operated server as part of the local-first design described here.

9. Children

Raily is a household utility, not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through support email, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

10. Data retention and deletion

  • Local App data remains until you delete it in the App, uninstall the App, or erase the device storage that holds it.
  • Support emails you send to us are retained only as needed to respond and operate support, then deleted or minimized when no longer necessary.
  • Apple may retain purchase records according to Apple's policies.

11. International users

Because processing is primarily on-device, your data residency for App content follows your device. If you contact support by email, that correspondence may be processed where our support mailbox is operated.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy. Material changes may also be noted in the App Store listing or in-App notice when appropriate.
